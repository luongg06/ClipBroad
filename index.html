    Where your clips live

    On your computer. That is the whole architecture.

    No server, no account

    Your history is written to Chrome's own storage.local on your machine. There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere for the text to go.

    Text only

    Images and files are never captured. Up to 20,000 characters per clip, and only what you actually copied — no formatting, no metadata.

    Yours to take out

    Export the lot to CSV, text, Markdown or JSON whenever you like. The JSON imports straight back, so leaving is as easy as arriving.

    Pause it any time

    Turn capture off in settings and the extension stops recording — useful before you copy a password. Favourites are never auto-deleted.
